The Tournament Courses

Pineapple Valley Golf Club Hua Hin, Springfield Royal Country Club & Black Mountain Golf Club will give you the ultimate golfing experience. You will be spoiled by Thailand's friendly caddies.

Pineapple Valley Golf Club Hua Hin

Opened in October 2008 to worldwide acclaim, Pineapple Valley Golf Club Hua Hin was named Asia's best new course in 2009. Boasting panoramic ocean and mountain views, pineapple patches, bougainvillea beds and of course, Banyan trees, come into play on this hillside course.

The clubhouse offers stunning vistas (especially at sunset) of the Burmese Mountains.

Black Mountain Golf Club

Black Mountain was transformed from lush jungle and pineapple plantations and located around 25 minutes west of Hua Hin. Set in a valley surrounded by huge black granite rock covered mountains, the Hua Hin golf course was the site for Asian PGA tour events in 2009 and 2010 and the European versus Asia match play Royal Trophy in 2011.

In 2012 and each year since Black Mountain was selected as one of the 100 best golf courses outside the USA by Golf Digest magazine.

The course has been set up for the serious golfer with tournament golf in mind. Since its opening in 2007, it has hosted both European and Asian Tour events, plus the prestigious Royal Trophy.

Springfield Royal Country Club

Springfield Royal Country Club offers a championship golf experience in the heart of Thailand's golf region. Known for its meticulous course maintenance and challenging layout, this course provides an exceptional golfing experience for players of all skill levels.

Features immaculate fairways, strategic water hazards, and stunning landscaping that creates a premium golfing environment.
